## STRATEGIC ANALYSIS

### What Content Format Wins?
1. **Carousels (5-9 slides) are KING.** @thestoicroad's top posts are ALL carousels. Long-form storytelling outperforms single-image quotes.
2. **Reels are the GROWTH ENGINE.** @thestoicroad's 36.7x outlier reel got 3.3 MILLION views. That is how you get discovered. Reels bring new followers; carousels keep them engaged.
3. **Single photo quotes are the WEAKEST format.** @stoicreflections relies on them and gets decent numbers, but their engagement-to-follower ratio is lower.

### What Monetization Works?
1. **Comment trigger words into DM automation** (ManyChat). @thestoicroad uses "Comment ROAD" to funnel people into a newsletter. We will use "Comment COMMIT" or "Comment LOCK" to funnel people to the CommitT app download page.
2. **Physical products work.** @stoicreflections sells a Memento Mori calendar. Proof that this audience opens their wallets.
3. **Series content drives follows.** @stoicsanity's "21 Rules" series creates FOMO — people follow so they dont miss the next part.

### Our Content Strategy (Derived from Outlier Data)
1. **Primary format:** 5-9 slide Carousels with long-form storytelling about discipline, dopamine detox, and phone addiction.
2. **Growth format:** Short Reels (under 30 seconds) with dark aesthetic, AI voiceover, and a hard-hitting one-liner hook.
3. **Retention format:** A numbered SERIES (e.g., "21 Days to Digital Freedom" or "The 10 Laws of Self-Control"). Each post ends with "Follow for Part X."
4. **CTA:** Every post ends with "Comment 'LOCK' and I'll send you the app that physically blocks your distracting apps."
