# Verified History

The **Verified** sub-tab provides a complete audit trail of past commitment instances. Every verification — whether successful or failed — is recorded here with timestamps, proof data, and outcome details.

---

## Record Structure

Each historical entry contains:

| Field | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Task Name** | The parent commitment title |
| **Instance Date** | The specific day this instance was scheduled |
| **Scheduled Window** | Original start and end time |
| **Outcome** | `VERIFIED` or `FAILED` |
| **Verification Time** | Exact timestamp when verification occurred (if successful) |
| **Method** | How verification was confirmed (GPS, photo, check-in, partner) |
| **Failure Reason** | Why the instance failed (if applicable) |

---

## Audit Purpose

This tab serves as an immutable accountability record. Users can review their own history to:

- Confirm that a past event was genuinely verified (not fabricated)
- Identify patterns in missed commitments
- Provide evidence to accountability partners that commitments were honored
- Track streaks and consistency over time

---

## Example

> The Verified tab shows the last week of a "Morning Gym" commitment:
>
> | Day | Outcome | Time | Method |
> | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
> | Monday | `VERIFIED` | 6:12 AM | GPS — Within 50m of Fitty Gym VIT |
> | Tuesday | `VERIFIED` | 6:08 AM | GPS — Within 50m of Fitty Gym VIT |
> | Wednesday | `FAILED` | — | Grace window expired (no verification attempt) |
> | Thursday | `VERIFIED` | 6:23 AM | GPS — Within 50m of Fitty Gym VIT |
> | Friday | `VERIFIED` | 6:15 AM | GPS — Within 50m of Fitty Gym VIT |
>
> Wednesday's failure triggered the embarrassing photo penalty. The waiver was not completed in time.
